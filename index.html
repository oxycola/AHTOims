Everything you need

Built for how grocery stores actually work

Every feature designed around real daily workflows — from the loading dock to the sales floor.

📦
Receive Shipments
Scan UPCs, enter cases received, and stock updates instantly. New items can be added on the spot. Supports both unit-based and weight-based receiving.
📋
Live Inventory
Full product database with category filtering, inline editing, and color-coded stock levels. Green, yellow, and red at a glance.
⚠️
Reorder Dashboard
Automatically populated when stock hits reorder points. Copy your entire order list to clipboard and paste straight into your UNFI portal.
⏱️
Expiration Tracking
Three-zone alerts: Expired, Expiring Soon, and Good. Print a weekly walk list grouped by aisle. Badge on the tab shows count at all times.
📉
Spoilage Log
Log expired, damaged, or donated product. Auto-deducts from inventory and tracks dollar loss over time so you can spot patterns.
⚖️
Weight / Scale
Full support for weight-based items like deli and bulk. Enter weight manually or connect a USB scale. Generate and print price labels instantly.
👥
Employee Board
Live break and lunch timers per employee, grouped by department. Overdue timers turn red automatically. Resets itself at midnight every day.
📈
Sales Trends
Daily bar chart, top 10 fastest movers, and category breakdown. Adjustable from 7 to 90 day windows so you can spot what to prioritize.
⭐
Special Orders
QR codes on shelf labels let customers submit orders from their phone — no app needed. Orders land in the system instantly for you to confirm.
